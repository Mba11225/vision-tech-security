An Analog DVR (Digital Video Recorder) uses coaxial or Cat6 cables with video baluns to bring video back to a central box. It is highly reliable, cost-effective, and standard for most residential and retail setups. An IP PoE NVR (Network Video Recorder) processes digital network streams over Ethernet (Cat6) cables. It supports higher 4K resolutions, intelligent AI perimeter alerts, and transmits both power and data on a single network cable.

Traditional infrared (IR) cameras switch to black-and-white night vision in the dark, which obscures vehicle colors, suspect clothing, and facial textures. ColorVu (Hikvision) and Full-Color (Dahua) technologies utilize ultra-large aperture lenses and soft warm white LEDs to produce vivid, full-color footage 24 hours a day even in zero ambient street light.
Storage duration depends on camera resolution, number of cameras, and compression format. With modern H.265+ compression:
  • 1TB HDD: ~12 to 15 days continuous recording for 4x 2MP cameras.
  • 2TB HDD: ~25 to 30 days continuous recording for 4x 2MP cameras (or 15 days for 4x 5MP cameras).
  • 4TB HDD: ~30 to 45 days for 8x cameras.
